Clip Stitch

Clips, in play order

Add or drop video files. They play top to bottom. Trim each one with the start and end boxes (seconds into that file).

Type a title on any clip to show it as a title card before the clip, or as a caption over its first seconds.

Drawing

Pause and draw: the drawing appears at that moment and stays for the time you pick under "Show for". Draw while it plays: your strokes are replayed exactly as you drew them.

Tick "Freeze frame" to stop the video on that frame while you draw. The exported video holds the still image, with your drawings, for the seconds you set, then carries on. The timeline shows freezes as striped blocks.

To remove one drawing, pick the Eraser and click it, or drag across several. The drawing under the eraser is outlined in red first. Undo brings back anything you erased.
