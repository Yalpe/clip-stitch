# Clip Stitch

Join short video clips into one video, draw on top, and add titles — all in your browser.

**Use it:** https://yalpe.github.io/clip-stitch/

- Add clips, reorder them, and trim each one.
- Draw with a pen, highlighter, or arrows. Draw while paused to place a drawing at that moment, or while playing to record the strokes as you make them.
- Give any clip a title: a full-screen card before it, or a caption over its first seconds.
- Export a single MP4 (or WebM) with sound.

Nothing is uploaded: your videos are processed entirely in the browser. Works best in Chrome or Edge. Export records in real time, so keep the tab in front until it finishes.

It's one self-contained `index.html` with no build step and no backend.
