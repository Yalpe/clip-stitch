# Clip Stitch

Join short video clips into one video, draw on top, and add titles — all in your browser.

**Use it:** https://yalpe.github.io/clip-stitch/

- Add clips, reorder them, and trim each one.
- Record your screen with microphone narration and computer sound; the recording lands in the clip list (Chrome or Edge on a computer).
- Draw with a pen, highlighter, or arrows, and remove any single drawing with the eraser (Undo brings it back). Draw while paused to place a drawing at that moment, or while playing to record the strokes as you make them.
- Add text boxes: click to type, drag to move, click again to edit.
- Freeze the frame while you draw: the video holds still with your drawings for as long as you set, then carries on.
- Give any clip a title: a full-screen card before it, or a caption over its first seconds.
- Mute playback while you edit; exports still keep their sound.
- Export a single MP4 (or WebM) with sound.

Nothing is uploaded: your videos are processed entirely in the browser. Works best in Chrome or Edge. Export records in real time, so keep the tab in front until it finishes.

It's one self-contained `index.html` with no build step and no backend.
